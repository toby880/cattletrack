Everything you need to run stockers right

Built specifically for stocker and backgrounding operations — whether it's wheat pasture, summer grass, or a dry lot — not adapted from a generic cattle app.

🚛

Load Receiving

Record every load as it comes in — owner info, head count, arrival weight from the scale ticket, purchase price, and cost basis. Ear tags entered as a series (1-150) or individually.

💊

Health Tracking

Log pulls, treatments, and death loss per animal or for the whole group. Built-in drug database with automatic withdrawal day tracking.

  • Individual or group treatments
  • Diagnosis and outcome tracking
  • Hospital pen status
  • Cost per head calculated
⚖️

Performance Tracking

Log periodic weights and the app calculates ADG automatically. See days on feed, current head count, and projected sale weight at a glance.

📊

Markets & LRP

Current Oklahoma cash prices, CME futures, and your daily LRP quote all in one place. The Sell Calculator tells you whether to sell now or hold another 30 days.

  • Cost of gain vs value of gain
  • Break-even price calculator
  • LRP rate entry and cost per head
  • USDA RMA calculator link
💰

Close Out

Enter sale weight and price at close out. The app calculates total revenue, profit/loss per head, and total health costs — everything you need for settlement.

📱

Works Offline

No signal in the pasture? No problem. Log health events and weights offline and everything syncs automatically when you get back in range.
