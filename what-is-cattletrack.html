What is CattleTrack Analytics?

CattleTrack Analytics is cattle herd management and decision-support software designed for commercial cow-calf and seedstock producers. It combines breeding records, production performance, fertility, genetics, structural evaluations, and cow-family data to help producers make breeding, selection, and culling decisions.

What It Does
Everything a herd's records and decisions run on

🐄 Animal Records

A complete profile per animal — pedigree, registration, tag history, and status.

💉 Health & Chute Work

Vaccinations, treatments, and chute-side scoring recorded right where the work happens.

🐂 Breeding & Calving

AI, natural service, ET/IVF, pregnancy checks, and calving outcomes in one connected record.

🧬 Genetics & EPDs

EPDs, pedigree, and a Genetic Intelligence score that turns your own herd data into an objective read on each animal.

⚖️ Production Performance

Weights, ADG, and weaning ratios tracked against your own herd average, not a generic benchmark.

🌾 Pastures & Sales

Rotation tracking, head counts by pasture, and sale records with settlement math built in.

Who It's For
Built for how different operations actually run

Commercial Cow-Calf Producers

Track the essentials — calving, health, weights, breeding — without paying for registration and pedigree tools you don't need.

Seedstock & Registered Operations

Full EPD sets, multi-generation pedigree, Genetic Intelligence scoring, and ET/IVF program management for registered herds making genetic decisions.

Stocker Operators

CattleTrack Stockers tracks loads, health pulls, death loss, ADG, and cost of gain for grazing and backgrounding operations.
